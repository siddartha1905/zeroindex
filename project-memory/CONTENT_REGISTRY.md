## Status Classification Vocabulary

* `Planned`: Curricular or editorial outline established; no production assets released.
* `Draft`: Working content files authored in local development.
* `Under Review`: Content undergoing faculty or peer academic verification.
* `Approved`: Formally authorized by faculty lead for public deployment.
* `Published`: Active and live on production.
* `Withdrawn`: Content removed or archived from public release.
